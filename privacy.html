Privacy policy

Google Drive access

The app requests full Google Drive permission through rclone. The sync workflow uses that permission to locate its configured folder, compare Drive file metadata, and upload new or updated encrypted vault files.

Storage and sharing

Cryptomator encrypts the files locally before rclone uploads them. Google Drive stores the encrypted vault in the authorized account. rclone stores OAuth credentials in its local configuration. The workflow has no separate application server and does not send vault contents or OAuth credentials to this website.

The website uses no analytics scripts or sign-in. Its host, GitHub Pages, may log visitor IP addresses for security.

Revoking access and removing data

You can revoke the app’s access in Google Account connections. You can remove uploaded files in Google Drive and remove the local authorization from rclone when it is no longer needed.
